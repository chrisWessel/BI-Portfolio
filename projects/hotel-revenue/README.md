# Hotel revenue dashboard

An interactive hotel performance dashboard focused on booking activity and revenue movement.

![Hotel revenue dashboard](../../assets/previews/hotel-revenue.png)

## Questions explored

- How do visits, revenue, costs, and profit compare with the prior year?
- How do booking outcomes and revenue vary by month and hotel type?
- Which market segments contribute to realized revenue or revenue loss?

## Tools

Power BI · Excel · Power Query · Data modelling

## Portfolio notes

The preview highlights year, quarter, month, weekday, customer, and hotel-type filters. It also compares hotel types, market segments, check-outs, cancellations, and no-shows. Source workbooks and report files are not included.
